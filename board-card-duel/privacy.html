Privacy Policy

Board & Card Duel (“the game”) is developed by Yunchieh Chen. The game does not collect, store or transmit any personal data.

  • No accounts, no ads, no analytics or tracking, and no in-app purchases.
  • Saves, settings, high scores, replays and your player look are stored only on your device and are removed when you delete the app.
  • Bluetooth and local network (Wi-Fi) are used only to connect directly with friends nearby to play together. Only game input and game state are exchanged; nothing goes through a server and nothing is kept.
  • The game does not use the camera, microphone, photo library or location.

If this policy changes, this page and the date above will be updated. Questions: Yunchieh0423@gmail.com
