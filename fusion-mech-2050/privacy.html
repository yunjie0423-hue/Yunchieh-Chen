2050聚變機甲　隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 11 月 22 日

《2050聚變機甲》（以下稱「本遊戲」）不收集、不儲存、不傳送任何個人資料。

  • 本遊戲不需要網際網路，沒有帳號、廣告、分析工具或第三方追蹤。
  • 存檔、設定、英雄榜與玩家 ID 只存在你的裝置裡，刪除 App 就會一併刪除。
  • 藍牙與區域網路權限只用來和附近的隊友連線合作，連線內容只有遊戲操作，不會傳到其他地方。
  • 錄音室功能錄下的聲音只存在你的裝置裡，只在遊戲中播放。

如有任何問題，請到支援頁面聯絡我們。

Fusion Mech 2050 — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Fusion Mech 2050 ("the game") does not collect, store or transmit any personal data.

  • The game needs no internet connection and has no accounts, ads, analytics or third-party tracking.
  • Saves, settings, the ranking board and your player ID stay on your device and are removed with the app.
  • Bluetooth and local network access are used only to play co-op with friends nearby; only game input is exchanged.
  • Voices recorded in the Voice Studio stay on your device and are only played in the game.

Questions? See the support page.
